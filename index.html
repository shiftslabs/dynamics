18 · Frequently asked

Questions, answered.

What is DynamicsLab?

DynamicsLab is Shift Labs' flagship Business OS, bringing multiple business functions into one connected software platform.

Is DynamicsLab the same as a collection of separate apps?

No. The goal is a shared operating layer where business records, workflows and context can work together.

Can Shift Labs build something custom?

Yes. If your needs do not fit the standard product, you can talk to Shift Labs about a focused or tailored software deployment.

Do I have to use every DynamicsLab feature?

No. You can adopt the capabilities that are relevant to your business and expand as your needs change.

Can I talk to someone before choosing?

Yes. Use the contact option and tell us what you are trying to accomplish.
